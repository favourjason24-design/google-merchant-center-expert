COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Not sure where to begin? Here are a few helpful starting points.

Can you help with a suspended Merchant Center account?+

I can help review the account status, identify visible policy or website issues and outline possible corrective steps. Reinstatement is determined by Google and cannot be guaranteed.

Can you work with an existing product feed?+

Yes. An existing feed can be reviewed for missing or inconsistent attributes, data quality issues and opportunities to improve its structure.

What do you need to get started?+

Usually your store URL, a short description of the issue and any relevant Merchant Center diagnostics. Never send passwords or verification codes.

Do you guarantee approval or sales?+

No. Google makes its own review and approval decisions, and sales depend on many factors. The focus is on careful setup, accurate data and actionable improvements.
